How We Work

From Idea To Launch

A consistent, transparent process whether the project is a single landing page or a full platform build.

01

Discover

We start with a call to understand your business, your users, and what "success" actually looks like for this project.

02

Strategy

We turn that into a scoped plan — what gets built, in what order, and what it will cost and take to ship.

03

Design

Wireframes and visual design, reviewed with you before a single line of code gets written.

04

Development

We build in visible stages, so you're seeing real progress throughout — not just at the very end.

05

Integration

CRM, payments, APIs and third-party tools connected and tested against real data.

06

Testing

Cross-browser, cross-device and performance testing before anything goes live.

07

Launch

A checklist-driven launch — DNS, SSL, backups and monitoring all in place before go-live.

08

Growth

Post-launch support, analytics review and a plan for what to build next.

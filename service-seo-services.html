What Is SEO?

Getting Found By People Already Looking

Search engine optimisation is the practice of structuring and writing your site so search engines understand what it's about, and rank it for the terms people are actually searching. Done properly, it's not a trick or a hack — it's fixing technical issues that hold a site back, writing content that answers real questions, and building the kind of site structure search engines can crawl and trust. It's slower than paid ads, but the traffic it brings keeps arriving long after you stop actively working on it.

Our Approach

What Our SEO Work Actually Covers

Website Audit

A full technical and content review to find what's actively holding your rankings back.

Keyword Research

Finding the terms your actual customers search, not just the ones with the highest volume.

On-Page SEO

Titles, headings, meta descriptions and content structure optimised page by page.

Technical SEO

Site speed, crawlability, indexing issues and structured data fixed at the source.

Content Optimisation

Existing pages rewritten or expanded to actually answer what people are searching for.

Internal Linking

A link structure that helps both visitors and search engines find your most important pages.

Performance

Page speed improvements, since it's both a ranking factor and a conversion factor.

Local SEO
